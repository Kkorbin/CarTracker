# Vehicle Search Criteria

The target: a clean-title 2021–2024 compact SUV, around 40k–60k miles, full modern
safety package, Apple CarPlay, mid-level-or-better trim, priced around $22k–$26k.

**RAV4 sweet spot:** a 2021–2022 RAV4 XLE, ~45k–60k miles, clean title, full safety
equipment, around $24k–$26k.

## Vehicle

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Compact SUV, roughly RAV4-sized |
| Primary targets | Toyota RAV4, Honda CR-V, Mazda CX-5, Subaru Forester |
| Alternatives | Similarly sized competitors acceptable |
| Model year | Prefer newer where it buys meaningful gains in safety, driver assistance, infotainment, or reliability |
| Drive type | AWD is a bonus, not mandatory — a FWD car can win on overall value |

## Budget

- **Target:** around $25,000.
- Value over sticker price: a $25–26k car with 45k miles beats a $21k car with 85k miles.

> Negotiating position — how far above target is acceptable, and the financing stance —
> is deliberately kept out of this repo. It lives in `NOTES.local.md`, which is
> gitignored and never published.

## Mileage

- Baseline assumption: **~11,000 miles/year** to judge whether mileage suits the age.
- **Preferred range:** ~45k–60k miles.
- Avoid cheaper examples at 80k–100k+ miles.

### Mileage quality bands

| Band | Stance |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | Preferred |
| Good / Normal | Preferred |
| Upper End | Acceptable if the price compensates |
| High | Usually avoid unless the discount is unusually strong |
| Very High | Usually avoid unless the discount is unusually strong |

## Title & history

- **Strong preference for a clean title.**
- Avoid salvage, rebuilt/restored salvage, flood, and other branded titles.
- Prefer no major accidents and ideally no significant structural damage.

## Safety & driver assistance

All strongly preferred:

- Apple CarPlay (essential)
- Adaptive cruise control
- Blind-spot monitoring
- Rear cross-traffic alert
- Automatic emergency braking / forward-collision mitigation
- Lane-departure warning and/or lane-keeping assist

Strong overall crash-test performance and a modern safety suite matter a lot.

## Trim

Avoid bare-bones trims that sacrifice the safety and comfort features above.

| Model | Minimum trim |
| --- | --- |
| RAV4 | XLE or higher |
| CR-V | EX or higher |
| CX-5 | Touring, Preferred, Grand Touring, or equivalent newer trim |
| Forester | Premium, Limited, Touring |

## Comfort & convenience

Desirable but **secondary** to safety, mileage, condition, and value: heated seats,
power liftgate, moonroof, leather/leatherette, power seats, memory seats, remote start,
nicer interior.

## Ownership

- **Reliability:** good long-term reputation and reasonable ownership costs.
- **Resale value:** important — avoid something cheap only because it resells poorly.

## Listings

- **Transparency:** prefer listings with a clear VIN, mileage, title/history, equipment
  list, and dealer pricing.
- **Investigate suspiciously cheap vehicles** for salvage history, accidents, hidden
  fees, rental history, or other explanations.
- **Links:** a direct vehicle-detail page, preferably the dealer's own listing. If no
  stable direct page exists, say so plainly rather than linking to a generic
  search-results page.

## Geography

Arizona, especially the Phoenix metro. Statewide Arizona is acceptable for a
compelling deal.
